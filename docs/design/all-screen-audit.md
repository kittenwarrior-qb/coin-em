# All-screen theme audit — 2026-10-11

## Scope and direction

The owner's approved Emotion Orbit and Kindness Workshop prototypes remain the visual anchors. Applied the frontend-design skill to hold their palette, typography, material and control hierarchy consistently, rather than inventing another direction. Orbit keeps indigo/lavender/berry with rounded readable Quicksand and thin outline chrome. Workshop keeps denim/coral/ivory paper with its cut-paper assets and legible supporting text.

Gallery: `brand-prototypes-v1/all-screen-review.html`. There are 106 captures (53 named review entries per theme, including related views of the same screen), plus 16 contact sheets. The gallery separates Home/room/popups, all 17 gameplay phases, and additional profile/card/public-page views. Click an image for its original full-resolution capture; public pages are captured full-page after their actual scroll-reveal and lazy image behavior.

Public full-page captures use the browser's actual reduced-motion preference, so off-screen reveal sections are readable in one image. No application animation styles are removed or disabled in production. The game/popup captures use normal motion.

## Refinements made

- Waiting-room and public-site logos use the approved theme wordmark, not the old white splash logo or Coin mark.
- Secondary white buttons use the quiet material instead of silently falling back to primary. Leave/close actions are visually distinct from start/confirm.
- Orbit settings/book/close chrome uses outlined icons; the paper theme retains its silhouette family.
- Orbit gameplay headings and gift title use the same rounded text family as the refined Menu.
- Night backgrounds use indigo or denim, not green; phase headings and round labels remain readable over the night treatment.
- History entries use theme surfaces, readable ink, a consistent book icon and a real circular close control.
- Reward rows and selected-state toggle use theme borders/surfaces/accent. Role identity badges and their colors are intentionally retained.
- The denomination icons in the gift picker use the same theme-specific star/face family as the wallet. Red and yellow selected tiles get appropriate tinting instead of inheriting a green selected surface.
- Paper avatar texture is clipped to the source silhouette, eliminating the rectangular noise artifact.
- Inventory close icon is dark on the pale circle. Input bounds remain visible before focus.
- Public card/guide pages and 3D viewer use theme backgrounds and readable UI typography. Original card faces, pairs, photos, tutorial illustrations and attribution graphics stay untouched.

## Verification boundaries

These are local presentation fixtures and real registered public pages, not live multiplayer sessions. Gameplay QA checks four viewer roles across 17 phases at compact and tall mobile sizes. Room/UI QA also checks desktop centering and reachable scroll controls. The supplemental admin capture uses an intercepted 401 to show the login form; no credentials, privileged overview, real room data or production mutation were used. This pass does not certify pixel-perfect replication, physical-device behavior, every keyboard/browser combination or a complete server-backed multiplayer game.

Reports are generated by `check-brand-themes.mjs`, `check-gameplay-themes.mjs` and `check-screen-completeness.mjs`, with screenshots published by `publish-screen-review.mjs`. Source changes preserve role eligibility, pending locks, Coin balances/rules, protected card artwork and action callbacks.

## Results

These results record the earlier all-screen pass. For the subsequent user-facing density,
image failure/retry, real-browser room/terminal verification and current release boundary,
see [user-ready refinement](user-ready-refinement.md). The gallery images were republished
after that pass; the older counts below are not a substitute for its evidence.

- Brand/room UI: 90/90 cases passed, at 320×400, 430×932 and 1280×900.
- Gameplay: 272/272 cases passed (two themes × 17 phases × four viewers × two mobile viewports).
- Supplemental screens/public routes: 126/126 cases passed, across the same three UI viewports.
- Final public-only recheck after overriding About's embedded legacy `!important` font lock: 30/30 cases passed. Gameplay and non-public selectors were unaffected by that narrowly scoped override.
- Component/navigation regression tests: 71/71 passed in ten files. TypeScript, scoped ESLint and production Vite build passed.
- Captures reviewed as 16 contact sheets, with focused full-resolution checks for Menu, forms, waiting room, history, inventory, rewards, coin picker, night gameplay and public-page headers. No missing-image, page-error, horizontal-overflow or unreachable-control failures were found by these checks.
