# EmCoin — Brand prototypes v1

9 visual concepts generated with the built-in image_gen tool, not the CLI. Created for comparison only; no production UI/assets changed.

## Evaluation

- A tier: direction B, Vòng Cảm Xúc. Best overall coherence across Home, Menu and board. Reduce orbit decoration and header height. Do not ship invented title/progress markers.
- B+ tier: direction C, Xưởng Tử Tế. Most distinctive material language; texture too strong and secondary colors compete with gameplay. Explore C2 with reduced texture if selected.
- B tier: direction A, Trao & Nhận. Friendly and readable at concept level, but menu still resembles generic pastel app UI.
- No S tier yet. These are subjective design judgments, not measured accessibility or user-test results.

## Guardrails / generated deviations

Not a logo redesign. EmCoin text is a concept identifier. No protected card faces generated. Numbered players are placeholders. Generated coin artwork/numbers are not approved production artwork, real balances or permission rules. B board introduces a title/progress dots; A/C introduce extra player colors; C uses stronger texture and more yellow than requested. All must be reconciled with actual UI before implementation. Dense board composition needs separate small-screen/10-player QA, contrast measurements and scroll testing.
